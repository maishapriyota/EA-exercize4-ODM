Watch a genetic algorithm pick the lights and the weekly specials, live, and compare it with plain random guessing that gets the same number of tries.

Stand-in problems. Your group's real black box can only be reached from your own computer. These simulations use practice problems built here, with a known best answer, so you can see how population size, mutation and crossover behave. Scores are shown as a share of the best possible answer.
